Privacy Policy for Tip Calculator

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Tip Calculator ("the Application") is an Android application developed by SLTGK. It helps you calculate a tip amount and a bill total. This policy explains what information is involved when you use the Application, and how that information is handled.

SLTGK is the developer named on the Application's Google Play listing. If you have questions, contact sltgk.dev@gmail.com.

1. We Do Not Collect or Transmit Your Data

   The Application is designed to function entirely offline.
   It does not access or use your device's internet connection for any functionality.
   It does not request internet permission.
   SLTGK does not collect, receive, use, or share the numbers you type, your saved tips, or any name you give a saved tip.
   The Application does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or photos.
   Nothing you create in the Application is transmitted by the Application to SLTGK or to any other third party.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

   The Application stores data only on your device, and only to provide the calculator:

   Working draft. As you type or change the tip percent, the Application saves the current bill, tip amount, tip percent, and total so that closing the app does not erase your work. Choosing "New tip" clears this draft.

   Saved history. When you choose Save, the Application stores a snapshot on your device. A snapshot may include an optional name you type (for example a restaurant), the bill, tip percent, tip amount, total, and the time you saved it. A name is optional. You can delete a saved snapshot from History. Saved snapshots are not edited in place.

   This on-device data is not an account. There is no sign-in.

3. Device Backup

   The Application allows Android backup. If backup is enabled on your device, the working draft and saved history may be included in your own device backup (for example a backup associated with your Google account) and restored onto a device that uses that backup. SLTGK does not receive that backup.

4. Data Collected by Google Play for Crash Reporting

   To help improve the stability and performance of the Application, we use the Google Play Console crash reporting feature.
   When a user has opted-in to automatically share usage and diagnostics data with Google at the Android system level, Google's services will automatically collect anonymized crash logs and other diagnostic information when the Application unexpectedly crashes.
   This information is collected by Google and then provided to us in an aggregated and anonymized format through the Google Play Console.
   The data we receive includes technical information such as:
   Stack traces from the app's code that help pinpoint the source of the crash.
   Information about your device (e.g., model, OS version).
   Other diagnostic data related to the crash event.
   We use this data solely for debugging purposes and fixing stability issues. This process helps ensure a smoother, more reliable experience for all users.
   For more information on how Google handles this data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party Access

   The Application does not share any user data with third parties.
   As stated above, any diagnostic data is handled by Google's services, which then provides an anonymized version to us.

6. Permissions

   The Application does not request internet access or any permission that would read personal or sensitive user data. It uses on-device storage only for the working draft and saved history described in this policy.

7. Your Choices

   You can clear the working draft with "New tip."
   You can delete a saved snapshot from History.
   Uninstalling the Application removes its local data from that device. Restoring a device backup may bring that data back.

8. Your Consent

   By using the Application, you consent to this privacy policy.

9. Changes

   If the Application's data practices change, we will update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will change.

10. Contact Us

   If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy or your use of the Application, please contact us at sltgk.dev@gmail.com.
